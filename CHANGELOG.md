# Wanderer Bags 0.1.0

First release, an extension of Wanderer (0.39 or later).

### New
- All your bags in one window drawn like every Wanderer window, following its profiles; the game's own item squares (right click, drag, tooltips, cooldowns, new items).
- Sorted by kind (just picked up, gear, consumables, crafting, quests, the rest, junk last, the free slots in one square) or bag by bag.
- A search by name or by kind; the item level on gear; Wanderer's green arrow on what is better for your branch; a coin on the junk; the game's sorting.
- The bank in its own window beside your bags at the banker: your character's tabs and the Warband's, the game's deposit and sorting, a right click to move an item between the two, the game's window one click away to buy or set a tab.
- Your bank anywhere: away from the banker, a link of your bags shows it as you left it.
- Its own page in Wanderer's options; one checkbox gives the game's bags back, another the game's bank.
