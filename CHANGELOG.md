# Wanderer Bags 1.2.14

### New
- The guild bank in a Wanderer window, beside your bags: the guild's tabs with what your rank allows in each and the withdrawals you have left today, the items of the tab shown laid out as in the game, with the search of the bags (each tab says how many it holds of what you search).
- Everything of the game's guild bank is there: take an item, put it in your bags with a right click, deposit from your bags with a right click, split a stack with Shift, drop gold on a square to deposit it, the item log, the gold log, the tab's text (written by those whose rank may), and a tab to buy for the guild master, with the game's own confirmation.
- The guild's gold and what you may still take of it today at the foot; deposit and withdraw ask through the game's own questions. What your rank would refuse is not offered: a short line says why.
- A link shows the game's own guild bank window, another one above it comes back; your choice is kept for this character. A new setting, "Wanderer's guild bank in place of the game's" (on), keeps the game's window if you prefer it.

### Fixed
- A search also looking in your other characters' bags: more than six found, the others counted (+3).
- At a merchant or the auction house, protected items keep Shift and a click (link, split a stack) and Ctrl and a click; only selling them is held back.
- Cooldowns show on items even where the game's newer buttons are missing; the bank always opens, even when the game is slow to show it.
- Alt+right click on an item is the game's again (use it on yourself); Alt+left click opens its card.
- An item Wanderer Auction turned into junk, common or better, always waits for your click at a merchant, even with Auction switched off.
- Nothing is moved into the bank while a merchant window is open.

### Changed
- Items in one of your sections show their own sign, the padlock is for kept items only; items a quest needs show the quest's grey sign.
- Click your gold at the bottom of the bags to take some in hand, for a trade or a letter.
- Clearer words: "Add to the shopping list", "Remove" a section, "Search an item...".
- An item one of your quests asks for shows the game's yellow question mark at its corner, easier to see than the grey one.
