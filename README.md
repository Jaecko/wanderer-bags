# Wanderer Bags

Your bags and your bank in one quiet window, made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 1.4 or later.

- All your bags in one Wanderer window, sorted by kind or bag by bag; items keep their place while the window is open.
- Quivers, ammo pouches, soul bags and profession bags each with their own free square.
- A search by name, kind, level (>40, 30-40), quality, BoE or equip slot, with ! for "without" and | for "or".
- The item level, "BoE", Wanderer's green arrow on what is better for you, a coin on the junk.
- Alt+click an item for a small card: keep it (never sold), set it aside for the auction house, mark it as junk or for the bank.
- Sections of your own, crafting reagents by profession, outfit pieces together; a section's card pins, moves, folds, sells or deposits it.
- At a merchant, a Sell button that asks first, goes by lots of 12 (what a merchant can buy back) and leaves aside what is worth far more at the auction house.
- The bank beside your bags at the banker (character and Warband tabs), what you marked put in on arrival, and viewable anywhere as you left it.
- Over an item, what your other characters carry; while you search, what they have of it.

Download: [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wanderer-bags). Pairs with [Wanderer Auction](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer-auction). More on [wanderer-addon.com](https://wanderer-addon.com).
