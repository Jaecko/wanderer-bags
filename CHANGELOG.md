# Wanderer Bags 1.2.0

Needs Wanderer 1.4 or later.

### New
- Alt+click an item for a small card: Keep, Auction, Junk, Bank or None (keys 1 to 5). Items marked Bank go into your bank at your next visit, one after another (an option, on; Shift as you speak to the banker skips it), or by the "Put marked in" button.
- Section cards: right click a section's title to pin it at the top, move it up or down, fold it, sell it, rename or remove it.
- Outfit pieces (Wanderer Outfits or the game's equipment manager) gather in a section of their own and are never sold; at the banker, a button brings the pieces an outfit lacks into your bags.
- Quivers, ammo pouches, soul bags and profession bags each have their own free square and count; the nearly full warning only looks at your ordinary bags.
- Items keep their place while the window is open: nothing slides under the mouse as you sell, use or deposit. Everything is sorted again when you open your bags.
- A richer search: levels (>40, <20, 30-40), qualities (rare, epic), BoE, equip slots (head, finger), ! for "without", | for "or", and the tooltip's words as a last resort. While you search, "All" in Select takes only what is found.
- "BoE" in the corner of gear that binds when equipped and is not bound yet (an option, on).
- New items can go straight to their own section with only the game's glow (an option, off).
- At the banker, a section's card deposits the whole section, or sends it to the bank at every visit ("Always to the bank").
- What your other characters carry: over an item, which of them have it, in their bags or their bank, as last seen (an option, on); while you search, the free slots' line says it too.
- Bags nearly full: the free slots' tooltip names what you carry worth least to a merchant.
- Your bags wider or narrower by dragging the bottom right corner, a row of squares at a time.

### Changed
- Selling a section asks first: how many items, for how much. Beyond 12 items (what a merchant can buy back), the sale goes by lots of 12, a click each; grey junk still goes at once.
- Items worth over three times more at the auction house are left out of a section's sale and named; a box sells them anyway.
- The game's Sell All Junk leaves aside what you keep, what is set aside for the auction house or the bank, your sections, outfit pieces and quest items.
- The look of every Wanderer window: a "Help" word, the kit's colors, sounds and cards; /wbags opens and closes the bags.

### Fixed
- Opening the bags no longer draws the window several times at once.
- Items the game had not described yet are drawn as soon as it does.
- The window is at least as wide as its title and buttons in every language; section titles never run under the Sell button.
- The bank seen from afar keeps your last good look at it, even when the game forgets its tabs.
