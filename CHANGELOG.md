# Wanderer Bags 1.2.7

### New
- Controller support: items are used with the bottom button and taken in hand with the top one; at a merchant, the right button uses the game's own bag key. Needs Wanderer 1.4.37 or later.
- With a controller, the left button opens an item's choices (Keep, Auction, Junk, Bank, your sections), as Alt + click does. Needs Wanderer 1.4.37 or later.

### Fixed
- No error any more when hovering an item placed in one of your sections.
- Renaming a section by changing only its letter case now works.
- "Sell all" no longer counts items that are never sold.
