# Wanderer Bags

Your bags and your bank in one quiet window, made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 0.40 or later.

- All your bags in one Wanderer window, sorted by kind or bag by bag, with a search.
- The item level, Wanderer's green arrow on what is better for you, a coin on the junk.
- Alt+click an item to keep it (never sold), set it aside for the auction house, mark it as junk or put it in a section of your own.
- Crafting reagents by profession, and a Sell link at merchants that never touches what you keep.
- The bank beside your bags at the banker (character and Warband tabs), and viewable anywhere as you left it.

Downloads: [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wanderer-bags) and [Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/wanderer-bags). Pairs with [Wanderer Auction](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer-auction). More on [wanderer-addon.com](https://wanderer-addon.com).
